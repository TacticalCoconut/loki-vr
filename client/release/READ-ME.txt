LOKI VR — Ragnarok Online in Virtual Reality: Player Guide
Package {{PACKAGE}} (Loki {{COMMIT}})

Loki VR lets you play Ragnarok Online in PC VR headsets (or desktop mode). It renders the world using the data.grf files
from YOUR own installed Ragnarok Online client (it never modifies your Ragnarok installation) and connects to any compatible
Loki Host (modified rAthena) or SpacetimeDB server.

1. WHAT YOU NEED
   - Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
   - A PC VR headset: Meta Quest (via Link cable, Air Link, Virtual Desktop, or SteamVR) or Valve Index / SteamVR headset.
   - A local Ragnarok Online client installation containing `data.grf`.

2. INSTALL & LAUNCH
   - Unzip this package into any folder (for example `C:\Games\LokiVR`).
   - Start your headset's PC VR link (or SteamVR) and open `LokiLauncher.exe` (or `Loki\Loki.exe`).
   - On first launch, select your Ragnarok Online folder (containing `data.grf`) and click Play.
   - In the bottom bar of `LokiLauncher.exe`, you can optionally toggle `Auto-Update: ON / OFF` if your server host distributes updates via GitHub Releases, or click `Rollback (vX)` after an update to revert to your previous version.

3. CHOOSING A SERVER & LOGGING IN
   - On the VR login screen, click `Servers…` to select a configured server or click `Add a server` to enter your server's IP/host and port.
   - Enter your account username and password (or create a new account if self-registration is enabled on the server) and press Log in.
